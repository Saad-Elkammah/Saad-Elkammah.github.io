Meeting Management

Multi-tenant meeting SaaS · For charities & associations

eiewan.com ↗

Meeting Management is a multi-tenant SaaS where charities and associations run governance meetings and elections, each in its own database, from scheduling to approved minutes.

Role
Full-Stack Laravel Developer. I built the meeting core.
Period
Aug 2025 — Jan 2026

Client-owned code: no source is shown here.

The problem

Companies and associations hold meetings and elections that have to stand up to scrutiny. Members are invited and identified, minutes are kept, and votes are counted without anyone seeing how an individual voted.

What I built

  • The platform: invitations, scheduling, agendas, electronic minutes, remote audio/video and file sharing, with customizable permissions per organization.
  • Secure voting: encrypted data, voter identification, aggregated results without exposing individual votes, and tamper-proof audit records.
  • Around the meetings: organisation sign-up with email OTP, a database per tenant, Moyasar checkout, live rooms on Agora with cloud recording, and AI minutes in Arabic with Laravel AI.
  • My contribution, the meeting core: the multi-step meeting builder, invitations and guest access, live quorum, voting and minutes approval.

Architecture

Built by me External service

Decisions and why

Aggregated results only
Organizations see the outcome of a vote, never how an individual member voted.
Tamper-proof audit records
Any change to the voting record can be detected, so the result can be trusted.
Permissions per organization
Each company or association decides who can do what.
One database per tenant
Each organisation gets its own database and /{tenant} URL, and central login hands over with a Sanctum token.
Paid before it opens
Moyasar payments are verified server-side, and every meeting route checks the subscription's expiry date.
